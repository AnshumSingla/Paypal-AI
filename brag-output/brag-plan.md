# Brag plan: Collective

**What it is:** a housing society buys groceries in bulk; each resident approves one PayPal hold for the most they'd pay, an AI Society Secretary gets suppliers to lower prices, and PayPal charges only the price the building unlocked.
**For:** residents of Indian housing societies (and the volunteer who runs the group order today).
**Sets it apart:** the PayPal hold is the agent's budget; the agent negotiates with money already authorized.
**Most impressive claim (from the product):** approved $3.91, paid $3.43.
**Visual hook:** forty flats lighting up one by one, then a price that keeps falling.
**Real UI shown:** the price ladder, the Secretary's message to a supplier and the reply, the receipt with PayPal IDs. Copy is the product's own; IDs are the app's stand-in IDs (fictional).
**Tone:** polished. Calm, premium, restrained; long holds, soft fades.
**Identity:** paper #FBFBFA, ink #1A1C21, muted #6B6F78, one indigo accent #2F3A8F, Mukta, logo = 3x3 grid of flats.
**Share caption:** Approve the max. Pay what the building unlocked.

## Music
100 BPM (beat 0.6 s), C major, soft pad (Cmaj7, Am7, Fmaj7, G6), round bass, gentle kick on 1 and 3, quiet hats, a soft pluck in key on each scene cut. Synthesised; no samples, no copyrighted music.
