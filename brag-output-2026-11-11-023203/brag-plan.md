# Brag plan: Collective

## What is this app?
A housing society buys groceries in bulk: each flat approves one PayPal hold for the most it would pay, an AI Society Secretary gets local suppliers to lower the price, and PayPal charges only the price the whole building unlocked.

## The angle
The money rail is the agent's budget. Everything on screen is real: the price ladder is a real supplier bid, the Secretary's message is the one it sent in this run, and the receipt shows the IDs PayPal's sandbox returned. The film is quiet and exact, like the receipt itself: approve the most, pay less, see the IDs.

## Hook (first 3.6 s)
Forty flats light up one by one on black, in PayPal blue, beside the line "40 flats buy the same rice, dal and eggs." then "One by one. At retail." (the README's own problem statement, trimmed).

## Key moments
- A real price ladder (Annapurna Kirana Mart, Large white eggs, 27+ $3.92, 39+ $3.69, 53+ $3.43): tiers light as neighbours join and "Price now" falls.
- Money on the table: the Secretary's real message to the supplier types out, and the supplier's real reply slides in.
- The receipt: Approved struck through, You paid, then three rows with real PayPal sandbox IDs.

## Outro / punchline
"Approve the max. Pay what the building unlocked." Logo and Collective. Small line: "PayPal holds, captures, refunds and payouts. Gemini runs the Society Secretary, with Groq as backup."

## User flow worth showing
Resident approves one hold → the Secretary negotiates with the authorized money → PayPal captures the lower price and releases the rest.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet, exact product film that reads like a well-made receipt
- Interpretation: six scenes, long holds, soft fades through black, one moving element per moment.

## Format: landscape, 1920x1080, 30 fps
## Duration: 28.8 s
Longer than the usual 25 s on purpose: the Secretary's real message is 25 words and the reply 13, and both must be readable at about 0.3 s a word.

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000000 (dark canvas), surfaces #0a0a0a, borders #1f1f1f
- Accent: PayPal blue #3d9bff on dark; the landing hero gradient (#001c64, #003087, #0059c9, #0070e0, #4cc3ff, #ffd140) once, for the reveal
- Text: #f5f5f5, muted #a3a3a3
- Fonts: Geist (display and body), Geist Mono (PayPal IDs), as in app/layout.tsx
- Strongest visual: the landing page's price card (Approved struck through, Charged in blue, tier rows) and the logo (3x3 grid of flats, two dimmed)
- The brief's paper-white, indigo and Mukta identity was the app's earlier look; the app has since moved to PayPal blue on black with Geist, so the film follows the app.

## Share copy (draft)
Approve the max. Pay what the building unlocked.

## Audio direction
- Role: no music (the user's call). Sound comes only from what happens on screen.
- Keystrokes (Kenney keyboard set, CC0) on every second character while the hook line and the Secretary's message type out, so it reads as typing, not a rattle.
- One soft mouse click when Ananya's PayPal hold is approved.
- Everything else is silent; silence between scenes is part of the calm.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4.2 s (0.0-4.2)
Grid of 40 flats on black; cells light in a scattered order. Text left-aligned beside it: "40 flats buy the same rice, dal and eggs." (held 3 s), then "One by one. At retail." (held 2 s).
Sequential: yes, 40 cells light over 2 s (non-text accent, not read).
Audio: keystrokes while the hook line types.
Transition: soft fade through black → 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 2.4 s (4.2-6.6)
The landing hero gradient fills the frame; the logo scales in; "Collective" and "Buy together. Pay less." (the landing headline, verbatim).
Audio: silent.
Transition: soft fade → 3

### Scene 3 — One hold, falling price — 6.0 s (6.6-12.6)
Left: "Each flat approves one PayPal hold." and "The most you'd pay. Neighbours join, the price falls for everyone." Right: the price card from the app: Large white eggs, 12 count; Annapurna Kirana Mart; "Approved in PayPal $3.92"; "PayPal hold approved" appears (one click), then the 27+ and 43-pack counts light the tiers and "Price now" falls $3.92 → $3.69.
Audio: one click for the hold.
Transition: soft fade → 4

### Scene 4 — Money on the table — 7.8 s (12.6-20.4)
Label "AI Society Secretary". The Secretary's real message from this run types out (about 3 s), then the supplier's real reply slides in at 16.9 s and holds 3.5 s.
Audio: keystrokes while the message types; silence for the reply.
Transition: soft fade → 5

### Scene 5 — The receipt — 4.8 s (20.4-25.2)
"Ananya's receipt": Approved (struck), You paid (big, blue), the egg price $3.92 → $3.52 a pack; rows at 21.5, 22.0 and 22.5 s: hold with its PayPal ID, capture with its PayPal ID, the rest released. A small "PayPal sandbox" chip.
Audio: silent.
Transition: soft fade → 6

### Scene 6 — Outro — 3.6 s (25.2-28.8)
"Approve the max. Pay what the building unlocked." Logo + Collective. Small line about PayPal and Gemini.
Audio: silent.

**Music:** none.
**Audio summary:** typing for the two lines that are typed, one click for the PayPal approval, otherwise quiet.
